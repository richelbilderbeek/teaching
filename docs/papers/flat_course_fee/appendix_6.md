# Appendix 6: the effect of management

I assume that management has the best intentions and that it
tries to help its training staff to the best of its abilities.
Due to this, I am likely to listen to their conclusions and ideas
and this may bias the willingness of other NBIS staff to do the same
(See [appendix 2](appendix_2.md) for the support for/against
a flat course fee within NBIS).

Unexpectedly, my trust in management does not match that of all NBIS colleagues.
Some have stated that decisions such as this are a one-way street.
I was given the advice to 'just obey'.
Even though this is a sentiment that I do think is worth investigating,
I did not ask for clarifications of this sentiment.

Again, I assume that NBIS Training has had the best intentions
when suggesting to introduce a flat course fee for online courses.
I even assume they are open to opposing ideas, such as this opinion piece.
